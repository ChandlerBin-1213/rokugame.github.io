Privacy Policy

Sky Strike
Effective date: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sky Strike handles information when you use the game on a Roku device.

Information We Collect

Sky Strike does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking software.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

Sky Strike stores limited game data locally in the Roku device registry so that your progress is available the next time you play. This data may include:

  • Campaign progress and unlocked stages
  • Coins, upgrade levels, scores, and earned stars
  • Selected abilities and sound settings

This data remains on your Roku device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties by Sky Strike.

Data Sharing

Because Sky Strike does not collect personal information or transmit game data, we do not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on the Roku device until it is removed through the Roku platform, such as when the app is uninstalled or the device is reset. Reinstalling the game may start a new save depending on the Roku device and account configuration.

Children's Privacy

Sky Strike does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Roku Platform Services

Roku may process platform-level information under Roku's own privacy policy and terms. That processing is controlled by Roku and is separate from Sky Strike.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the publisher contact listed on Sky Strike's Roku Streaming Store page.
